Support

Contact: open an issue at github.com/marker2601/petri-siege-site/issues. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

A purchase did not arrive

Open Supply from the title screen and tap Restore purchases. The Lab License comes back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. ATP grants are credited once, at the moment of purchase; if one was charged but not credited, contact us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving progress to another device

Progress is stored only on your device. Open Records, copy the save export text, and paste it into Records on the other device.

No sound

Check the ring/silent switch and the volume sliders in Settings. Sound starts after your first tap in the game.
