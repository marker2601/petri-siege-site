Support

Contact support

Open a support request (free GitHub account needed): github.com/marker2601/petri-siege-site/issues/new?template=support.yml

We answer support requests within 5 working days. Requests are public, so do not include personal details.

A purchase is missing

Open Supply from the title screen and tap Restore purchases (also in Settings > System). The Lab License and purchased ATP come back on this device. If something is still missing, open a support request with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving progress to another device

Progress is stored only on your device. Open Records > Transfer, copy the save string, and paste it into Records > Transfer on the other device. Purchases are not part of the save string: on the other device, use Restore purchases.

No sound

Check the ring/silent switch and the volume sliders in Settings. Sound starts after your first tap in the game.
