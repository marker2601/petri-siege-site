Petri Siege

Privacy policy

Effective 21 September 2026. This policy covers the Petri Siege app for iPhone.

In short: Petri Siege does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send anything to us.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your iPhone: research, unlocked defender classes, banked ATP, settings, records, a suspended run and a list of purchase transaction IDs that stops a purchase from being credited twice. This data never leaves your device. You can copy it yourself with the save export in Records.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, payment method or billing address. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Information Apple may share with developers

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix bugs. We cannot identify you from them.

Support requests

Support requests are issues in a public GitHub repository. Anything you write there is public and is processed by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement, so do not include personal details. Requests stay on GitHub until they are deleted, so that other players can find answers. You can delete your own issue at any time, or ask in the issue for us to delete it, and we will.

Deleting your data

To delete your progress, use Records > Transfer > Reset progress (purchased ATP you have not spent stays), or delete the app, which removes everything it stored. Purchases are recorded by Apple on your Apple Account; we cannot see or delete that record.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
